Finals prep — Asaf scouting & spell sequencing

Built from the CRL archive through 2026-08-18. All lift figures are measured against a constraint-aware baseline and corrected for multiple comparisons (Benjamini-Hochberg, q=0.10). Only statistically surviving rows are shown.

How to read this

Lift is preference, not arithmetic. Cards cannot repeat inside a duel set, so a game-1 Lightning + Barb Barrel mechanically guarantees game 2 has neither. Comparing against overall spell popularity would just rediscover that rule. Every baseline here is renormalised over only the packages still legal given what earlier games burned, so a 3x lift means they genuinely prefer it among what remains available.
Significance. Hundreds of context/outcome cells were scanned, so an uncorrected p<0.05 would be meaningless — roughly 15 false hits per 300 rows. Each row gets an exact binomial test against its legal-pool baseline, then Benjamini-Hochberg FDR at q=0.10. Rows that failed are excluded from this artifact entirely.
What this cannot tell you. These are population-level tendencies across the whole tracked roster, not reads on one specific opponent. Sample sizes per cell (the n column) range from 14 to 546 — prefer high-n rows. And a surviving pattern is a correlation: it does not prove the player is reasoning the way you'd guess.
Asaf's entropy figures are unreliable at this sample size. With 16–74 duels most win-condition transitions occur once, and a table of one-counts always looks maximally random. The concentration percentile is the trustworthy metric; treat entropy as directional only.
A related test came back null. Whether an opponent won or lost game 1 does not measurably change which win condition they bring next (296 cells scanned, 0 survived FDR). So don't re-plan your read just because game 1 went badly.
